# Privacy

Structura runs entirely on your device.

- **No account, no sign-in.**
- **No network calls** for capture, reconstruction, optimization, or export.
  Nothing about your scans leaves the phone unless *you* share or save a file.
- **Camera + depth** are used only to build the scan you're capturing.
- **Photos access** is requested only when you choose "Save render to Photos,"
  and is used only to write that one image.
- **Files you export** go where you send them via the system share sheet
  (Files, AirDrop, another app). Structura doesn't upload them anywhere.

There is no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting network call in the app.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why | When |
|-----------|-----|------|
| Camera | See the scene to capture depth | During a scan |
| Photos (add) | Save a render you asked to save | Only on "Save to Photos" |
